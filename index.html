Permission Center (Single Entry Point)

One unified screen for RBAC + ABAC. Configure permission profile, role assignment, conditional access, and validation in one guided flow without switching tabs.

1) Define Permission → 2) Assign Role → 3) Add ABAC Conditions → 4) Validate Access → 5) Save Policy
